Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Welcome to Unstory. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Unstory is designed to help you reflect, process emotions, and gain clarity safely. We intentionally minimize data collection.

1. Information We Collect

Unstory may collect the following types of information:

  • User Input: Responses you write inside the app (stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated).
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics such as session count or feature usage.
  • Device Information: Basic technical information (device type, OS version) for app performance improvements.

2. How We Use Information

  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Fix bugs and enhance performance
  • Understand anonymous usage trends

3. Data Storage

Your written reflections are stored locally on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud backup or syncing (if available in future versions).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

4. Third-Party Services

Unstory may use third-party services such as analytics providers, crash reporting tools, or advertising networks (if applicable).

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Unstory is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including access, correction, or deletion of your data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
